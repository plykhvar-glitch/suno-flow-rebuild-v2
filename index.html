The Apple Ads path

One app, very different searches. One paywall per search.

Your Apple Ads bring in people with very different reasons to pay. Someone who typed “make songs” wants to make a lot of music. Someone who typed “SoundCloud” or “BandLab” already makes or posts music and cares about releasing it. Someone who typed “ai cover” wants their voice on a track. Today all of them see the same paywall. Here is how the same flow can speak to each of them.
